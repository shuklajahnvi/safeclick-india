ONLINE SHOPPING

Suspicious websites

Look for misspelled domains, unrealistic prices, unclear contact details and unusual payment demands.

Check a website →

Five warning signs to check

01

Unexpected urgency
Messages threaten account closure, penalties or missed rewards unless you act immediately.

02

Requests for secrets
Someone asks for your OTP, PIN, password or payment credentials.

03

Unusual links or addresses
The domain is misspelled, shortened or unrelated to the organisation named.

04

Too-good-to-be-true offers
Prizes, jobs or returns require upfront payment or confidential details.

05

Pressure to bypass normal steps
You are told to install remote-access software, move platforms or keep it secret.

Pause. Verify independently. Then act.

Don't use the phone number or link inside a suspicious message to verify its claim. Find the organisation's official website or app yourself.

02 / PROTECT

Lock down your accounts.

A few repeatable habits can make it harder for someone to access your email, social accounts and personal information.

01

Use unique passwords

Don't reuse passwords across email, banking, shopping and social accounts. A reputable password manager can help.

02

Enable two-factor authentication

Add a second verification step where available. Consider passkeys or authenticator-based options when offered.

03

Review account access

Check recovery details, sign out unknown devices and keep apps and operating systems updated.

How to secure your WhatsApp account

  • Enable two-step verification and review linked devices.
  • Never share your SMS registration code with anyone, even if they claim to be support.
  • Verify unexpected requests for money by calling the person on a known number.
  • Adjust profile and group privacy settings to your comfort level.
Never share your OTP, password or PIN.

Legitimate support staff should not need you to reveal secret authentication codes.

03 / VERIFY

Pay with confidence.

Digital payments are convenient. Knowing what you're approving helps you use them more safely.

UPI fraud prevention: the basics

Before approving a UPI transaction, read the payment screen carefully. Confirm the recipient name, amount and whether the action is a payment or a request to collect money.

Entering your UPI PIN authorises a payment.

You do not need to enter your UPI PIN simply to receive money. Never share your PIN or OTP with callers who claim to be support staff.

Before every digital payment

  • Verify the recipient name and amount.
  • Read the action carefully; don't confuse a collect request with money being sent to you.
  • Don't follow caller instructions blindly, even if they claim to be bank staff.
  • Install payment apps from trusted app stores and keep them updated.

How to check if a shopping website is genuine

  • Check exact domain spelling before entering payment details.
  • Look for a clear business identity, contact details and return policy.
  • Search independent reviews beyond the seller's own website.
  • Be cautious if the only payment method is a transfer to an unknown person.
